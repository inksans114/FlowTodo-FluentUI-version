## Original celebration sound

`reward.wav` is an original synthetic confetti pop and ascending bell flourish,
generated by `tools/make_reward_sound.py`. It contains no sampled voice or music
and follows this repository's license. Played once after an explicit completed
action, with separate on/off control and respect for the notification mute.
